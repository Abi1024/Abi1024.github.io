About

I build high-performance C++ systems for streaming and out-of-core graphs. At Stony Brook I led the implementation of GraphZeppelin and CubeSketch: a streaming engine that applies millions of edge updates per second and computes connectivity on dense, dynamic graphs that do not fit in RAM. I also supervised undergraduates on that codebase. I am looking for backend, storage, and infrastructure roles that use algorithms and systems software.
